Our story

We learned property from the people who own it.

Vintrell began in marketing. Working side by side with established landlords showed us how property really works — and where the real opportunity lies. We've been building on that ever since.

  1. Chapter I Where it beganWe started Vintrell as a marketing business — running online stores and helping landlords put their properties in front of the right guests and tenants.
  2. Chapter II Learning from experienceThat work brought us alongside landlords with large portfolios. Seeing first-hand how they buy, manage and grow opened our eyes to the property world, and to how much it can deliver when it's done properly.
  3. Chapter III Into propertySo we committed to it: studying property as a whole — strategy, the numbers and compliance — and launching Vintrell Property to source deals for investors.
  4. Today AI at every stepAI has been part of our journey from day one — from running online stores, to marketing landlords' properties, to finding the strongest deals today. Now we build those same systems for other businesses.
