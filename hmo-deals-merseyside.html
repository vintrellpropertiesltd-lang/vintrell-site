Existing HMO or conversion?

There are two ways into an HMO, and they carry different risks.

An existing, lawful HMO

Already let by the room, already licensed, with a rent roll you can check. You pay more for it, but you’re buying an income from day one. The key question is whether its use as an HMO is lawful in planning terms, not just whether it has a licence.

A conversion

A large house that becomes an HMO after works. The buy price is lower and the uplift is bigger, but the deal depends on planning, the layout and the build. Where the council has an Article 4 direction for HMOs, a conversion needs planning permission first.

Why a licence isn’t proof of planning use

An HMO licence is about management and safety. Planning is a separate system. A house can hold a valid licence and still not have lawful planning use as an HMO. That matters when you buy, refinance or sell.

Lawful use usually comes from one of these:

  • a certificate of lawful use for a small HMO (class C4);
  • planning permission for a large HMO of seven or more people (sui generis);
  • evidence that the HMO use started before an Article 4 direction came into force in that area.

Where the paperwork isn’t there, we tell you before you spend money on legals.

What we check on every HMO deal

Floor area and room sizes

EPC floor area, then each room against the national minimums: 6.51 m² for one adult and 10.22 m² for two.

Planning history

Past applications, certificates of lawful use and whether an Article 4 direction applies to the street.

Licensing

The council’s licence register, the licence conditions and when it’s up for renewal.

Flood risk

Checked against the national flood maps.

Council tax

The banding for each flat or the whole house, which changes the bills you’re covering.

The numbers

Room rents for that street, running costs, costed works and a walk-away price.

Questions we get asked

Do you source licensed HMOs that are already let?

Yes. We source existing HMOs with a rent roll as well as houses with conversion potential, and check licensing and lawful planning use on both.

Is an HMO licence the same as planning permission?

No. A licence covers management and safety. Planning use is separate, and a licensed HMO can still lack lawful planning use. We check both before a deal reaches you.

What are the minimum HMO room sizes?

Nationally, a bedroom for one adult must be at least 6.51 m² and for two adults at least 10.22 m². Councils can set further standards in their licence conditions.

Do you source blocks of flats?

Yes, freehold blocks across Merseyside, checked for title, council tax banding per flat and the condition of the building.
